Method

How it is counted

  1. Dashboards follow the territory owner on the Dynamics user record (Sales Manager - P21). The buttons under each name list the territories that dashboard covers.
  2. An account's territory is the P21 rep number carrying most of its Financial Lines sales; accounts with no sales use the number in their customer code (Calgary 604 and 610 codes count as 704 and 710).
  3. Sales come from Financial Lines by P21 rep number (fiscal year November to October, through the last month loaded, against the same months last year). Orders, quotes, Mining and cases reach a territory through the account.
  4. Quotes "brought in" are quotes where the person is Manager. A Mining opportunity becomes the quote's opportunity when inside sales converts it (its type changes from Mining to Quote or Order), so "opportunities opened" counts everything the person created and "converted" counts those now a quote or order. Open Mining and activity follow the record owner. In the work lists, the person's own quotes, Mining and cases join in on their full view; a single territory button shows that territory's accounts only. Activity, Mining opened and quotes brought in on the scorecard are measures of the person and do not change with the territory buttons.
  5. Visits use appointments (customer name in the subject matched when Regarding is empty) and completed phone calls by anyone. Follow-up logged = an email the Manager sent from Dynamics on the quote (outside sales only).
  6. Order values are reliable from January 2026; values that are really an order or quote number are ignored.
